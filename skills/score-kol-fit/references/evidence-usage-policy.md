# S06 Evidence Usage Policy

Use this before collecting, citing, storing, or rendering creator evidence.

## Evidence Classes

```text
public_creator_profile
  Public handles, platform pages, public posts, public media profiles.

public_content_sample
  Public videos, captions, reviews, posts, articles, live clips, product demos.

user_private_creator_list
  Treat as restricted. Do not expose names, contacts, rates, or negotiation notes in public HTML unless approved.

internal_creator_performance
  Treat as private. Report derived conclusions and confidence, not raw confidential rows, unless approved.

creator_rate_or_budget_inputs
  Treat rate cards, quotes, negotiation notes, product seeding costs, and campaign spend as restricted unless the user approves public rendering.

upstream_handoff_evidence
  Prefer evidence IDs from S01-S05 handoffs. Do not reopen full artifacts unless needed.
```

## Public Search Policy

Use web/MCP search only when:

```text
creator candidates must be verified
local creator ecosystem is unknown
category creator norms affect the recommendation
brand safety risk needs public evidence
candidate content samples need public source refs
creator discovery needs local query/source coverage
```

Search targets may include:

```text
search engine results for local language category queries
public platform pages
YouTube/TikTok/Instagram public profiles where accessible and permitted
creator websites/media kits when public
specialist media/reviewer sites
local forums and communities
retailer editorial, PDP, Q&A, marketplace creator, and buying-guide pages
affiliate, deal, comparison, coupon, and price-watch sources
public articles/interviews
brand safety or disclosure sources when public
```

Respect platform terms, access restrictions, privacy, and robots. If a source cannot be accessed, record the access gap.

## Discovery Coverage

For public creator discovery, record:

```text
queries attempted
source strata attempted
accessible sources
blocked or partial sources
candidate count by source stratum
candidate count promoted to scoring
why candidates were excluded
```

Do not claim a local creator ecosystem is complete. Use coverage language such as "visible public candidates found through accessible sources".

## Metric Caution

Treat public metrics as proxies:

```text
followers
views
likes
comments
engagement rate
subscriber count
posting frequency
```

Do not infer true demographics, conversion, fake-follower status, or sales lift without first-party or audited data. Budget and outcome ranges must cite whether the basis is first-party data, public proxy, benchmark, or hypothesis.

## HTML Safety

Public HTML may render:

```text
creator archetypes
candidate IDs or approved public names
scores and risk summaries
source refs
brief tasks
test backlog
budget and expected outcome ranges after private rates are aggregated or approved
```

Public HTML should not render:

```text
private creator contacts
rates
contracts
raw internal performance
private blacklist notes
restricted campaign details
raw rate cards, quotes, or negotiation notes
```
