# Marketing Skills Adaptation Map

This file records what is borrowed from `coreyhaines31/marketingskills` and how it
is changed for GTM Master. The goal is method transfer, not copying SaaS-specific
workflow.

| Source skill | Keep | Hardware adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| product-marketing | Product context, JTBD, objections, switching dynamics, customer language, proof points | Move into S00 Project Brief and optional intake; support B2C products without B2B persona assumptions. |
| customer-research | VOC extraction, source weighting, confidence labels, digital watering holes | Use local forums, review sites, marketplaces, YouTube, TikTok, app stores, NSS/NPS, support, and previous generation feedback. |
| competitor-profiling | Raw data snapshot, comparable competitor profiles, source-first synthesis | Extend to local e-commerce, retail pages, carrier/operator offers, expert reviews, previous generation, and price-band jump rivals. |
| marketing-plan | AARRR, current-state scoring, progress state, budget logic, S-curve thinking | Convert to hardware GTM readiness, recoverable state machine, hidden S13 validation, and dashboard report state. |
| pricing | Van Westendorp, MaxDiff, value-based pricing, pricing psychology | Add COGS/BOM, channel margin, finance/installment, MSRP vs street price, promo policy, and local-only private calculator. |
| launch | ORB owned/rented/borrowed launch channels, phased launch | Adapt to owned store/CRM, rented marketplaces/search/social, borrowed KOL/media/retailer/operator audiences. |
| ab-testing | Hypothesis format, sample size thinking, guardrail metrics, ICE | Keep inside hidden S13 validation plan; mark synthetic persona tests as hypothesis generation only. |
| copy-editing | Seven sweeps, proof, specificity, zero-risk checks | Use in S05 for editable text and in S14 as a Chinese dashboard readability and proof-visibility audit. |
| ads | Platform selection, retargeting, creative testing hierarchy, CPA/ROAS review | Use as MKT investment and channel testing logic, not as guaranteed sales forecast. |
| analytics | Tracking plan, event naming, validation checklist | Convert to launch measurement readiness, retailer/DTC split, UTM, and sell-in/sell-through tracking. |
| sales-enablement | Objection docs, one-pagers, ROI/value props | Convert to retail guide, operator pitch, KAM one-pager, and channel objection handling. |
| consumer-decision-journey | Consideration, evaluation, purchase, post-purchase experience, loyalty loop | Use as the journey backbone for local search terms, competitor interception, proof assets, retail/PDP moments, setup/use experience, and earned review/referral signals. |
| retail-market-measurement | Price, availability, retail presence, shopper touchpoints, and market signal triangulation | Use to distinguish local market facts from model inference: named retailers, price-comparison sites, official channels, marketplaces, reviews, forums, video comments, and user-supplied sell-through data. |

## Things Not Directly Reused

- SaaS-only churn, seat pricing, trial paywall, and SDR lifecycle rules are kept
  only when a hardware product has subscription, paid app, service plan, or
  sales-led channel triggers.
- Directory submissions and Product Hunt tactics are optional launch ideas, not
  core hardware GTM evidence.
- AI image/video generation is optional and does not override the user's rule
  that uploaded fixed visuals may be treated as non-editable.
