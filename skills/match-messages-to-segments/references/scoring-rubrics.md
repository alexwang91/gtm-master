# S03 Scoring Rubrics

Scores are 0-100 unless stated otherwise. Clamp final scores to 0-100.

## Message-Market Fit Score

```text
Message-Market Fit =
  Scenario Priority * 0.18
+ Segment Relevance * 0.16
+ Product-Job Fit * 0.14
+ Benefit Clarity * 0.12
+ Proof Readiness * 0.12
+ Objection Coverage * 0.10
+ Local Language Fit * 0.08
+ Competitive Distinctiveness * 0.06
+ Evidence Confidence * 0.04
- Claim Risk * 0.08
- Overpromise Risk * 0.06
```

Interpretation:

```text
75-100 = lead message candidate
55-74  = support or test message
35-54  = weak message hypothesis
0-34   = do not use
```

## Proof Readiness Score

```text
Proof Readiness =
  Proof Availability * 0.25
+ Proof Relevance To Scenario * 0.20
+ Source Quality * 0.15
+ Local Trust Fit * 0.12
+ Competitor Comparison Support * 0.10
+ Asset Usability * 0.10
+ Claim Safety * 0.08
```

## Objection Severity Score

```text
Objection Severity =
  Frequency * 0.20
+ Purchase Impact * 0.20
+ Trust Barrier * 0.16
+ Price or Risk Sensitivity * 0.14
+ Competitor Pull * 0.12
+ Return or Support Risk * 0.10
+ Evidence Confidence * 0.08
```

## Claim Risk Score

```text
Claim Risk =
  Regulated or Sensitive Category * 0.22
+ Proof Gap * 0.20
+ Absoluteness of Wording * 0.16
+ Local Compliance Uncertainty * 0.12
+ Product Experience Dependency * 0.10
+ Competitor Comparison Risk * 0.08
+ Support/Warranty Delivery Risk * 0.07
+ Evidence Confidence Inverse * 0.05
```

Interpretation:

```text
75-100 = high risk; do not use without review
55-74  = medium risk; use cautious wording and proof
35-54  = manageable with proof notes
0-34   = low risk
```
